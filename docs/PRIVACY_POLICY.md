# Privacy Policy for Expenses

Effective 18 October 2026. Applies to the Expenses app for Android and iPhone ("the app"), published by Chandrashekhar Nelluri ("I", "me").

**In one sentence: everything you enter stays on your phone, the app has no way to send it anywhere, and I collect nothing about you.**

## What the app stores
The expenses, incomes, accounts, categories, budgets, bills, notes and settings you enter, and the bank or payment alerts you choose to let it read (see below). They are stored in a database on your phone, encrypted on Android with a key kept in the phone's secure hardware (Android Keystore) and, on iPhone, protected by iOS Data Protection.

## What I collect and share
Nothing. The app has no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking, and it contains no third-party software that collects data. **The Android app does not have the internet permission**, so it cannot send data off your phone even by mistake. I do not sell or share any data, because I do not have any.

## Things you can choose to switch on
- **Reading bank texts (Android, only in the version installed directly, not the Google Play version).** If you turn it on and grant Android's permission, the app reads each incoming text on your phone only to decide whether it is a transaction or a bill from a bank, card or biller. Texts from people, one-time passwords and unknown senders are discarded without being kept; only a count is recorded (for the "How capture is doing" page). A transaction you do not accept is kept in the review list until you delete it.
- **Reading payment-app notifications (Android).** If you turn it on and give Notification access, the app reads notifications only from Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and BHIM, to find a payment. Notifications from every other app are ignored without being read.
- **Bank messages on iPhone.** iPhone does not let any app read messages. If you set up a Shortcuts automation, or paste a message, the app reads that message on your phone with the same rules as above.
- **Reminders.** Bill and budget reminders are created on your phone from your own data. Nothing is sent to a server.
- **App lock.** The app asks your phone's fingerprint, face or passcode check. The app never sees your fingerprint or face.
- **Files you choose.** Export (CSV or Excel), backup and import use files you pick or save yourself. A backup can be protected with a passphrase you choose; if you forget it the file cannot be opened, by me or anyone. Where you save or send a file (Drive, Mail, AirDrop) is your choice and that service's own policy applies to it.

## Permissions (Android)
Notifications (reminders), fingerprint or face unlock (app lock), start after restart and short background work (reminders and scheduled export), and, only in the directly installed version, receiving texts. Notification access is a setting you turn on yourself. None of them sends data anywhere.

## Retention and deleting your data
Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete entries in the app, wipe everything with **Start fresh**, or uninstall the app, which removes its data. Because I hold no copy, I cannot recover or delete anything for you.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and I do not knowingly collect anything from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes I will update this page and the date above. The app will not start collecting data without a new version and a clear notice.
