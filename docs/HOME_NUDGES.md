# Home nudges: one card at a time

Home used to stack every card that had something to say: a bills banner, the To review banner, "money to get back", "N expenses have no category" and the "Getting started" checklist, one under another, above the budget and the entries. On a small screen that was most of the first page. Now **one** card is drawn, the first of the order below that is present and not put off. What else is waiting is one line under it ("Also waiting: Expenses with no category. See More"), and every one of them is still a card or a row on the More page.

Code: `HomeNudge`, `HomeNudges` and `NudgeSnooze` in `core/domain/.../HomeNudges.kt` (pure, host-tested by `HomeNudgesTest`); drawn by `EntryListScreen` in `shared/.../ui/Screens.kt` with `NudgeCard` (`shared/.../ui/NudgeCard.kt`, one look for every nudge, see below); the put-off times are read with the other small settings on every rebuild (no query over entries).

## The order (highest first; the enum order of `HomeNudge` is the order)
| # | Nudge | Present when | Put off by |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bills due (`BILLS_DUE`) | a tracked bill, a repeating item that waits for the person, or a card statement is due within a week or overdue | "Not now" (a close button on the card): 24 hours, or until more bills are due than when it was put off |
| 2 | To review (`INBOX`) | texts, alerts or e-mails wait in To review | same |
| 3 | New bills (`BILLS_NEW`) | bills were found in messages and nothing is due this week | same |
| 4 | Money to get back (`WAITING`) | expenses are marked as expected back | same |
| 5 | No category (`CATEGORISE`) | 3 or more expenses have none (capped at 500) | "Not now": its own 7 days (unchanged) |
| 6 | Set up reading bank texts (`SETUP`) | the guided capture setup was offered (at the end of the first tour) or begun and left, the phone has a way to capture, and nothing is capturing yet (no capture switch on; on iPhone, no message has reached the app) | "Not now": 24 hours (the usual put-off); "Hide": for good (the setup stays in More, Capture) |
| 7 | Getting started (`FIRST_WEEK`) | the first-week checklist has steps left | "Hide": for good (unchanged) |

Why this order: money and dates first (a due bill costs a fee or a lapsed policy), then what only the person can place in the books, then tidying, then the capture setup (one step that makes later ticks of the checklist happen by themselves: a person with something real waiting sees that first, and on a new install nothing is above it), and the checklist last because it is for someone with nothing else to do yet. A card that is put off lets the next one through; one that is put off comes back after the day, or at once when more arrives (a new text, another bill due), so a put-off is never a way to miss something new.

## One look and one close button (Home Option B, Slice 112)
Every nudge is drawn by the same `NudgeCard`: the same tinted surface (the accent at 10% over the page), 20 dp corners, 16 dp from the screen edge, a title, ONE supporting line, ONE button on the right ("Open", "Review", "Add", the setup's own word) and ONE close button (a cross, 48 dp). There is no "Not now" / "Hide" text button any more. What the close does is the nudge's own and is unchanged: a day for To review, bills, new bills, money to get back and the setup (the same `NudgeSnooze` rules); a week for "No category" (`UncategorisedNudge`); for good for "Getting started" (a screen reader says "Hide Getting started for good"). The setup is the one nudge that had two ways out: its close is the day; **right after it, a bar says "Hidden for today" with "Hide for good"** (the same setting as the old Hide button; the setup stays in More, Capture). The cards no longer list the first bills or To review items (they used to add up to 140 dp): they say how many and how much, and the whole card opens the list. The "Also waiting" line now carries the counts ("Also waiting: To review (3), Expenses with no category (5). See More"; `HomeNudges.alsoWaitingLine`). At large text (150% and up) the button goes under the words so nothing is squeezed into a one-word column.

## What is not a Home nudge, and why
- **The budget block and its safe-to-spend line**: part of the Budget section the person arranges in Customise home; it is a figure, not a request.
- **Due today** (the Bills section in Customise home): posting a repeating item is that section's own job.
- **The Welcome card** on an empty Home, which is what the empty page is made of.
- **The backup reminder** and **the iPhone build's "stops opening in N days" notice**: More page only (not on Home). More shows **one** of them: the signing notice outranks the backup reminder (`HomeNudges.moreNotice`; a build that stops opening makes a backup moot; the backup reminder is still in Your data).

- **The trip card (Travel mode, Slice 110; `docs/TRAVEL_MODE.md`)**: while a trip is on today, **one compact line right under the month card**, "Trip: Goa · day 3 of 7 · ₹4,500.00 spent", the whole card opening the trip's page. It is **information, not a request**: it is not in `HomeNudge`, is never put off, never counts in "Also waiting", and does not take the place of a nudge, so the rule "one nudge at a time" is unchanged (`TripModeUiTest.theCardIsNotANudgeSoOneNudgeIsStillAllThatShowsAndTheOthersAreOneLine` shows the trip card, the one nudge and the "Also waiting" line together). Order on Home: month card, **trip card (when a trip is on)**, the one nudge, the "Also waiting" line, then the sections of Customise home. A trip that is over, still to come or switched off has no card. It is one line (wraps at large text, at least 48 dp high); amounts are masked with Hide amounts. It is read with the other small facts on every rebuild: the few enabled trips, and, only while one is on, one aggregate over its own days (no scan of entries). Its More row is **Travel mode** (Money).

## The More page
The cards at the top of More (at most two are drawn) follow the same order and are never put off: Bills (due this week, else new), To review, Money to get back, No category. A card put off on Home is therefore always one tap away there; each also has its row below.
