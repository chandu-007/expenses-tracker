# Home nudges: one card at a time

Home used to stack every card that had something to say: a bills banner, the To review banner, "money to get back", "N expenses have no category" and the "Getting started" checklist, one under another, above the budget and the entries. On a small screen that was most of the first page. Now **one** card is drawn, the first of the order below that is present and not put off. What else is waiting is one line under it ("Also waiting: Expenses with no category. See More"), and every one of them is still a card or a row on the More page.

Code: `HomeNudge`, `HomeNudges` and `NudgeSnooze` in `core/domain/.../HomeNudges.kt` (pure, host-tested by `HomeNudgesTest`); drawn by `EntryListScreen` in `shared/.../ui/Screens.kt` with `NudgeCard` (`shared/.../ui/NudgeCard.kt`, one look for every nudge, see below); the put-off times are read with the other small settings on every rebuild (no query over entries).

## The order (highest first; the enum order of `HomeNudge` is the order)
| # | Nudge | Present when | Put off by |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bills due (`BILLS_DUE`) | a tracked bill, a repeating item that waits for the person, or a card statement is due within a week or overdue | "Not now" (a close button on the card): 24 hours, or until more bills are due than when it was put off |
| 2 | To review (`INBOX`) | texts, alerts or e-mails wait in To review | same |
| 3 | New bills (`BILLS_NEW`) | bills were found in messages and nothing is due this week | same |
| 4 | Money to get back (`WAITING`) | expenses are marked as expected back | same |
| 5 | No category (`CATEGORISE`) | 3 or more expenses have none (capped at 500) | "Not now": its own 7 days (unchanged) |
| 6 | Set up reading bank texts (`SETUP`) | the guided capture setup was offered (at the end of the first tour) or begun and left, the phone has a way to capture, and nothing is capturing yet (no capture switch on; on iPhone, no message has reached the app) | "Not now": 24 hours (the usual put-off); "Hide": for good (the setup stays in More, Capture) |
| 7 | Getting started (`FIRST_WEEK`) | the first-week checklist has steps left | "Hide": for good (unchanged) |

Why this order: money and dates first (a due bill costs a fee or a lapsed policy), then what only the person can place in the books, then tidying, then the capture setup (one step that makes later ticks of the checklist happen by themselves: a person with something real waiting sees that first, and on a new install nothing is above it), and the checklist last because it is for someone with nothing else to do yet. A card that is put off lets the next one through; one that is put off comes back after the day, or at once when more arrives (a new text, another bill due), so a put-off is never a way to miss something new.

## One look and one close button (Home Option B, Slice 112)
Every nudge is drawn by the same `NudgeCard`: the same tinted surface (the accent at 10% over the page), 20 dp corners, 16 dp from the screen edge, a title, ONE supporting line, ONE button on the right ("Open", "Review", "Add", the setup's own word) and ONE close button (a cross, 48 dp). There is no "Not now" / "Hide" text button any more. What the close does is the nudge's own and is unchanged: a day for To review, bills, new bills, money to get back and the setup (the same `NudgeSnooze` rules); a week for "No category" (`UncategorisedNudge`); for good for "Getting started" (a screen reader says "Hide Getting started for good"). The setup is the one nudge that had two ways out: its close is the day; **right after it, a bar says "Hidden for today" with "Hide for good"** (the same setting as the old Hide button; the setup stays in More, Capture). The cards no longer list the first bills or To review items (they used to add up to 140 dp): they say how many and how much, and the whole card opens the list. The "Also waiting" line now carries the counts ("Also waiting: To review (3), Expenses with no category (5). See More"; `HomeNudges.alsoWaitingLine`). At large text (150% and up) the button goes under the words so nothing is squeezed into a one-word column.

## What is not a Home nudge, and why
- **The budget block and its safe-to-spend line**: part of the Budget section the person arranges in Customise home; it is a figure, not a request.
- **Due today** (the Bills section in Customise home): posting a repeating item is that section's own job.
- **The Welcome card** on an empty Home, which is what the empty page is made of. While it shows (a brand-new install, before Skip or the first entry) the month card, "Set a budget" and the safe-to-spend tiles are not drawn above it, and a dimmed **Example month** (`ui/ExampleMonth.kt`, constants in `core/domain/ExampleMonth.kt`) is drawn below its buttons. The example is only a drawing: it is never stored, totalled, exported, shown in a widget or notified (`ExampleMonthNeverStoredTest`, `UiS6FirstRunTest`). "Hide example" sets `example_hidden`; the tour is opt-in ("Show me around", five stops, `Guide.shortPlan`).
- **The backup reminder** and **the iPhone build's "stops opening in N days" notice**: More page only (not on Home). More shows **one** of them: the signing notice outranks the backup reminder (`HomeNudges.moreNotice`; a build that stops opening makes a backup moot; the backup reminder is still in Your data).

- **The trip card (Travel mode, Slice 110; `docs/TRAVEL_MODE.md`)**: while a trip is on today, **one compact line right under the month card**, "Trip: Goa · day 3 of 7 · ₹4,500.00 spent", the whole card opening the trip's page. It is **information, not a request**: it has a progress bar since Slice 112 (the share of the whole trip budget spent, or how far through the days it is; no bar and no percent under Hide amounts), it is not in `HomeNudge`, is never put off, never counts in "Also waiting", and does not take the place of a nudge, so the rule "one nudge at a time" is unchanged (`TripModeUiTest.theCardIsNotANudgeSoOneNudgeIsStillAllThatShowsAndTheOthersAreOneLine` shows the trip card, the one nudge and the "Also waiting" line together). Order on Home: month card, **trip card (when a trip is on)**, the one nudge, the "Also waiting" line, then the sections of Customise home. A trip that is over, still to come or switched off has no card. It is one line (wraps at large text, at least 48 dp high); amounts are masked with Hide amounts. It is read with the other small facts on every rebuild: the few enabled trips, and, only while one is on, one aggregate over its own days (no scan of entries). Its More row is **Travel mode** (Money).
- **The capture heartbeat chip (UI S6, U-11; `CaptureHeartbeat` in `core/domain`, host-tested by `CaptureHeartbeatTest`)**: one small line right under the trip card, "Last bank text seen yesterday 8:14 pm" (iPhone: "Last bank text through Shortcuts ..."). It shows only while capture is on (a capture switch on Android; on iPhone once a message has come through a Shortcut or a paste) **and** a bank text was ever seen; with neither there is no chip. After more than 5 days with no bank text it turns amber (never red) and says so in words on a second line ("No bank text for 8 days. Tap to find out why."). One tap opens the capture checkup ("Why isn't capture working?"). It is **information, not a request**: not in `HomeNudges`, never put off, not counted in "Also waiting". What is stored is one setting, `capture_last_seen_at` (epoch milliseconds, written by `MessageIngest.noteBankText` when a message from a bank reaches capture: a transaction, a bill, or a bank's one-time-password, declined or non-transaction text; a text from a person or from a sender not on the list does not count); nothing of any message is kept. Home reads that one setting in `refresh()` (and the capture switches only when it is set). Tags: `capture_heartbeat` (+ `capture_heartbeat_text`, `capture_heartbeat_note`).

## The More page
The cards at the top of More (at most two are drawn) follow the same order and are never put off: Bills (due this week, else new), To review, Money to get back, No category. A card put off on Home is therefore always one tap away there; each also has its row below.

## Tags
`inbox_banner`, `bills_banner`, `waiting_banner`, `categorise_nudge` (+ `categorise_nudge_review`, `categorise_nudge_later`), `first_week_card` (+ `first_week_dismiss`, now the cross) are unchanged. The buttons are `inbox_banner_open`, `bills_banner_open`, `waiting_banner_open` (new; the whole card still opens too), the bills line is `bills_banner_line`. New (guided setup, Slice 107): `setup_nudge` (+ `setup_nudge_title`, `setup_nudge_line`, `setup_nudge_open`, `nudge_notnow_setup`; `setup_nudge_hide` is gone: the "Hide for good" bar after the close replaces it). The open Getting started card also has two quiet links, `first_week_checkup` (while its capture step is not done) and `first_week_earlier`. The trip card (Slice 110) is `home_trip_card` (+ `home_trip_line`); it is not a nudge and has no "Not now". New: `nudge_notnow_inbox`, `nudge_notnow_bills`, `nudge_notnow_waiting` (the close buttons), `home_more_nudges` (the "Also waiting" line), `open_categorise` (More row and card).

## Pause nudges and occasional milestones (UI S6, owner decision D2)
Notifications has, at the top, **Pause nudges** (`pause_nudges_switch`; lengths `pause_nudges_week`, `pause_nudges_month`, `pause_nudges_forever` "Until I turn it back on"; a line `pause_nudges_status` "Paused until 25 Nov" / "Paused") and **Occasional milestones** (`milestones_switch`, off by default). Both are on the iPhone build too (only the notices are not there). Settings: `nudges_paused_until` (epoch milliseconds; `Long.MAX_VALUE` is "until turned back on"; absent, 0 or a time in the past is not paused), `milestones_on` ("1"), `milestones_seen` (`id@epochDay` joined by commas), `milestones_since` (when the milestone clock started, or "old"). Code: `NudgePause`, `Milestones`, `NoSpendDays` (`core/domain`, host-tested), `MilestoneFigures` (the ledger side, `shared`), `Moments.kt` (the card, the Pause rows, the Save tick).

**What a pause covers** (decided here, one rule: a suggestion waits, a commitment or something the person asked for does not):
- Home cards: To review, New bills, Money to get back, No category, Set up reading bank texts, Getting started (`NudgePause.HOME_PAUSED`). To review and New bills are reminders to look at a list that stays one tap away in More with its count, so they wait too.
- The backup reminder (the card in More and the dot on the More tab) and the milestone card.
- Push notices that only suggest: a bill found in a message (`BILL_FOUND`), "spending is ahead of the month" (`BUDGET_PACE`), the month-end note (`BUDGET_WRAPUP`) and "card bill is paid" (`CARD_CLEARED`), held back in `NotificationService.check` (nothing held back is recorded as sent).

**Not paused:** Bills due on Home (a bill due or overdue is a commitment with a date), push reminders for a tracked bill, a repeating expense or a card's due date, a card's statement and credit-limit notices, budget near/over alerts, the weekly recap (off until the person turns it on), the cards at the top of the More page (the person went there on purpose), and the trip card.

**The milestone card** (`milestone_card`, `milestone_text`, `milestone_thanks`): not a nudge (it asks for nothing, has no close or put-off, is not in "Also waiting", and sits under the nudge area), one line, the drawn `SuccessCheck` and "Thanks". Ids: `first_entry` (a new ledger, first two weeks), `first_week` (7 to 35 days of use, at least 3 entries), `entries_100`, `first_backup`, `under_budget:<month start>` (N-26: the month that ended under its overall budget, only with a monthly budget that had a limit; text "{Month} ended under budget."), `quiet_days:<month start>` (a month with at least 5 no-spend days). Caps: each id once ever, at most 6 in 365 days, at least 14 days between two, never while paused, never while the switch is off. No streak, no counter, no amount in any text (so nothing to hide under Hide amounts), no push notice and no new permission. It is worked out with the slow figures (settings first; the ledger is read only when the rules allow one; the month facts once a day), never in `refresh()` or on the save path; "Thanks" records it as seen.

**Success ticks** use the shared `SuccessCheck` (a tick drawn over 280 ms once, complete at once under Reduce motion): on Save, in the bar that says "Added ..." (`save_success_check`, `save_success_text`; only an entry that was added, not a delete or a type change), and on Paid (`bill_paid_check_<id>`, now the shared tick, kept for about 1.2 s). The Calendar marks past days without spending, from the ledger's first entry on, with a small ring (`calendar_no_spend_<day>`; the cell says "no spending") and the summary line `calendar_no_spend_summary` ("6 no-spend days this month").
