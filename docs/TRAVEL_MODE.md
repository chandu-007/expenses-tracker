# Travel mode (Slice 110)

All names, amounts and dates in tests and screenshots are invented. **Nothing here was run on a real phone, an iPhone or with TalkBack.** (Not to be confused with the older "Travel mode" of Slice 80, the add form's "Paid in another currency": that one is unchanged and is about one expense in another currency with a rate you type. This one is about a trip.)

## What it is, in plain words
You tell the app about a trip: a name, the first and the last day, and a tag (the app suggests "Trip: Goa"; you may type any tag). While the trip is on, every **expense** you add that is **dated** on one of those days gets the trip's tag by itself. You can take the tag off any entry and it stays off. Later, Search (by tag and days) and the Reports (by tag) show everything the trip cost, and the trip's own page adds it up: total, days, average a day, a bar for each day, where it went, and a daily budget if you set one.

Decided by the owner and built as asked: **one tag per trip**; **no foreign-currency handling** (the ledger stays in its one currency; nothing is converted); phone and tablet/fold two-pane.

## The model
`Trip` (`core/domain/.../Trip.kt`): `id`, `name` (at most 40 characters), `tag` (an ordinary tag: at most 30 characters, no commas), `startDay`, `endDay` (**both included**; calendar days counted since 1970-01-01 as the Search dates are, so a trip means the same days in whatever zone the phone is in), `enabled` (default on), `dailyBudgetMinor` (optional, 1 to 100,000,000,000 minor units). Days must lie between 2000-01-01 and 2099-12-31 and a trip lasts at most **366 days**; a person keeps at most **200** trips.

Stored in one small table, `trip` (`Schema.sq`; `SchemaUpgrade.ensureLatest` creates it and its index `trip_days` with `IF NOT EXISTS`, like `entry_replaced`, so it is safe on every open and on a database that has it already). Every change is logged to `op_log` like any field change (`entity "trip"`). No other table, column or index changed (`SchemaFrom0106Test` pins that). Chosen over settings rows because the rows are typed, the overlap check and the day lookup are one indexed read, and the pattern is the one the last released schema change used. Code: `TripRules` (pure rules and the form's words), `TripSummaryMath` (pure figures), `TripStore` and `TripTagging` (`core/storage/.../TripStore.kt`), `LedgerStore.trips`.

### Days and time zones (IST-safe, tested)
A day is a **calendar day in the phone's zone**, read when an entry is made: an entry is inside a trip when its time is at or after 00:00 of the first day and before 00:00 of the day after the last day (`SearchDates.fromMillis/untilMillis`). So 00:00 on the first day is inside, 23:59:59.999 on the last day is inside, 00:00 on the day after is outside, 1 ms before the first day is outside. India has no daylight saving, so every day there is 24 hours; for a zone that has it the day is 23 or 25 hours (tested with London's change days). The same instant can be inside a trip in one zone and outside it in another (a phone that travelled): the entry's own date is the date in the zone the phone is in when it is made, as everywhere else in the app. The trip's days are not shifted by the zone.

### No overlap
A trip that is **on** may not share a day with another trip that is on. Saving (or switching on) one that does is refused, nothing is changed, and the message names the other trip: **These dates overlap your trip "Goa" (3 Nov – 9 Nov 2026), which is on. Change the dates, or turn that trip off.** A trip that is off may sit on any days. The store checks it again whatever the caller did, and a restore never makes two that are on overlap (it keeps the later one switched off).

## The auto-tagging rule (one hook, creation time only)
**Applied once, when the entry is created, by the entry's own date (not by when it was typed).** The hook is in `LedgerStore.save`, the one function every entry is written through: when the entry does not exist yet and the caller passes `EntryDetails(tripTag = true)`, it asks `TripStore.tripAt(occurredAt)` (one read of the few enabled rows and a comparison of days; no scan of entries; measured below) and adds the trip's tag in the same transaction, in the spelling the ledger already uses for that tag, after the tags the person typed. An entry that already has ten tags is left as it is. **An existing entry is never tagged by the hook** (an edit of the date, amount or note, an Undo, a restore): so a tag taken off stays off, and no per-entry "opt-out" record is needed. The default is `tripTag = false`; each path that records new spending on a trip asks for it, so a path added later does not tag by accident.

Only **expenses** are tagged. Never: income, transfers (card bill payments included), lent and borrowed, a friend's payment, **repeating items** posted by the app, **bills** marked paid ("Record the payment" in Bills and a bill payment text in To review: a bill is not spending on a trip), restored entries, entries made by a type change.

### Every way an entry is created (and what it does)
| Path | Code | Tagged? |

### Refunds (decided)
A refund of an expense that carries a **trip tag** is given that tag when it is created, whatever its own date and whether the trip is still on: the tag is the one of the trip that covered the **expense's** day and that the expense carries (found among all trips, on or off). So Search by tag shows the refund beside its purchase. The reports already counted a refund with the tags of the purchase it reverses (`spendByTag`), so the trip's total **nets out** either way; the tag on the refund only makes it visible in Search. A refund of an expense that is not tagged, or whose tag the person took off, gets nothing; neither does one made after the trip was deleted (the reports still net it out). The trip's figures count a refund on the day it is received, like every report: a refund that arrives after the trip's last day is outside the trip's days (it is in the month it arrived).

## "Tag N earlier entries" (explicit, counted, with Undo)
Never automatic. After a trip is **created**, or its **days grow** (or it is switched on again), the app asks: **Tag N earlier entries from these dates?** with the number of live **expenses** on the days the trip **gained** that do not carry the tag (an expense you took the tag off inside the days the trip already had is not offered again). Yes tags exactly those in one step (history shows an edit of the tags) and shows **Tagged N entries "Trip: Goa"** with Undo, which takes the tag off exactly the entries it was put on (one the person took it off meanwhile is left as it is). Not now changes nothing. The trip's page has the same action for the whole of the trip, **Tag N earlier entries** (shown only when N is more than zero); its question says an expense you took the tag off yourself is tagged again, because that action is yours and counted. An entry that already has ten tags is left out and the bar says how many. Code: `TripTagging.offer/apply/undo`, `TripRules.newlyCovered`; tested in `TripStoreTest` (offer contents, exact Undo, ten tags, only the gained days, history).

**Delete a trip** asks first and says so: *Only the trip goes. The tag "Trip: Goa" stays on the entries it was put on, so Search and Reports still show them. No entry is changed.* **Ending a trip early**: change its last day in the form, or **End trip today** on its page (asks first; only while the trip is on and has days left).

## Backups (`docs/BACKUP_FORMAT.md`, section `trips`)
An optional payload key `trips` (`id`, `name`, `tag`, `startDay`, `endDay`, `enabled`, `dailyBudgetMinor`), **left out when there are none**, so a backup of a ledger without trips is byte for byte what it was; an older reader ignores it (the tags are on the entries as always). A restore merges by id, makes every trip fit (or leaves it out), keeps a trip that would overlap one that is on here switched off, reads at most 20,000 of a file, keeps at most 200 and never tags an entry. Sealed layers (`EXPENC1/2/3`) carry it inside the payload like everything. Tested in `TripBackupTest` (round trip, no key when none, older reader, older eras, restoring twice, overlap, hostile lists of 5,000 and of 100,000 entries, sealed files) with `BackupFormatDocTest` and `BackupErasRegressionTest`.

## Performance
The hook is one read of the enabled trips (a table of a handful of rows) plus a comparison; with no trip at all it is one read of an empty table. No cache: it cannot go stale (another connection, a restore) and costs less than a hundredth of a millisecond. Home reads the enabled trips on every rebuild and, **only while a trip is on today**, one aggregate over the trip's own days (it walks the spend lines of those days and looks each one's tag up by key, so a tag used widely elsewhere costs nothing); the trip pages are rebuilt only while open. Measured on the host (JVM, 20,000 entries, a third tagged): the hook 0.006 ms per call with no trip, 0.008 ms with one, 0.012 ms with five; a trip's lines 18 ms, its totals 16 ms, the offer 11 ms; a save with the hook asked for and no trip +0.02 ms (`TripPerformanceTest`). **The device save-path A/B against 648a179 (`PerformanceBudgetTest` / `RefreshBudgetTest`) was NOT measured** (see `docs/PHASE2_PROGRESS.md`, Slice 110): from the code, a save adds one read of an empty or tiny table, and Home's rebuild adds one read of the enabled trips.

## Decisions and limits (so the owner can overrule them)
- **The page counts tagged entries inside the dates** (what "See all entries" shows). An entry tagged for the trip but dated outside its days (a flight paid a month before) is not in the total; it is in Search by tag alone. Widen the dates or accept it.
- **A refund that arrives after the trip ends** is outside the trip's days (see above).
- **Search's line "N results · ₹X spent" does not take refunds off** (it adds up the expenses it lists; this is how Search always counted, and a tagged refund now appears in its list); the trip's page does net refunds out. With a refund in the trip the two figures differ by the refund (seen in a screenshot: Search ₹13,400.00, page ₹11,900.00 with a refund of ₹1,500.00). Left as it is; the owner may want Search to net refunds too.
- **Two trips may use the same tag** (a yearly trip): the days tell them apart.
- **The whole-trip "Tag earlier entries" tags again** an expense whose tag was taken off inside the days (it says so and counts). The question after a trip is saved or its days grow does not.
- **A tag the person typed that looks like the trip's tag** is merged (ignoring capitals), not doubled.
- **An entry with ten tags** gets no trip tag (no room); the person's own tags come first.
- **No foreign-currency handling**; the trip's money is the ledger's currency, as everywhere.
- Deleting a trip never touches entries; a deleted trip's tag is an ordinary tag.
- The widget and the iPhone's extensions run in the app's process or read the same database; the hook reads the trips on every save, so a trip made a moment ago is seen at once.
